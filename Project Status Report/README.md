# 🎶 Fresh Beats — Business Intelligence Report

**A Report-Based BI Analytics Project**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of a Business Intelligence Analyst training program. The goal was to analyze user behavior and music trends on **Fresh Beats** — a fictional online music streaming platform that also serves as a promotional launchpad for emerging artists — and to deliver a stakeholder-ready status report with actionable recommendations.

> **Role:** Business Intelligence Analyst (Beginner)
> **Tools:** Data visualization analysis, report writing
> **Deliverable:** Stakeholder report with insights, visuals, and recommendations

---

## 🏢 Company Background

Fresh Beats is a dynamic online platform that:
- Provides a **music streaming service** for general listeners
- Acts as a **promotional catalyst** for up-and-coming musicians
- Generates revenue through **artist partnership deals** for promotional exposure

---

## 🧩 Business Challenges

The project addressed three core strategic challenges:

| # | Challenge | Description |
|---|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | **Artist Promotion Effectiveness** | Evaluate whether current promotional strategies deliver maximum ROI for artists |
| 2 | **User Engagement & Conversion** | Understand what drives free-to-paid user conversion and long-term retention |
| 3 | **Genre Promotion for Emerging Artists** | Improve content recommendations by identifying trending genres and user preferences |

---

## 📊 Key Findings

### 🎵 Genre Trends
- **Pop** peaked in 2021 and has been on a downward trend since
- **Electronic** and **Hip-Hop** are on a sustained upward trend
- **Rock** is the most popular genre among free users — a potential growth segment

### 👥 User Engagement
- **Paid users** are significantly more active than free users, listening to more tracks overall
- Free-to-paid conversion represents a major opportunity for revenue growth

### 📈 Visuals Included in Report
- *Average Number of Users by Genre over Time* — highlights genre momentum shifts
- *Tracks Listened by User Tier over Time* — illustrates the engagement gap between free and paid users
- *Listens by Genre and User Tier over Time* — maps genre preferences across subscription tiers

---

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Catalog Expansion** — Solicit Electronic and Hip-Hop artists and offer time-limited free promotional deals to grow these high-trend genres
2. **Targeted Marketing** — Run focused campaigns aimed at the typical demographics for Electronic and Hip-Hop fans
3. **Referral Program** — Introduce a referral incentive (e.g., free trial of paid tier) to accelerate user conversion
4. **Intermediate Subscription Tiers** — Lower the barrier to paid conversion by introducing mid-level pricing options
5. **Rock Segment Analysis** — Investigate retention and engagement strategies for rock listeners, given their dominance in the free user base
6. **Promotional Deals** — Offer time-limited upgrades and other promotions to convert free users into paid subscribers
